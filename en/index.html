Faces of the journey

Characters on extreme paths.

Some seek answers; others survive what was done to them. What unites them is the crossing: each character must face a different boundary within their world.

A young woman from Braen raised among scarcity, surveillance, and walls. Her need to understand what lies beyond the border may lead her to dangerous truths.

About the author

Imagination was the beginning.
Persistence made the universe grow.

Willian Quirino writes fantasy and science fiction set in worlds shaped by conflict, memory, survival, and transformation.

His stories began in handwritten childhood journals and returned to life years later as illustrated books, original soundtracks, and expanding worlds.

For Willian, a story is not confined to the page: it can also be experienced through images, sound, and emotion.

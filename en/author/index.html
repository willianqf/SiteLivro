Willian Quirino

Author, illustrator, and composer

Lost stories.
Worlds rediscovered.

Willian Quirino writes fantasy and science fiction shaped by conflict, memory, survival, and transformation. His journey began in childhood, before he fully understood the scale of the worlds he was creating.

The earliest pages of Elemental were handwritten in a 2006 journal, when Willian was about ten years old. The first manuscripts of Veter appeared in another journal in 2008.

After suffering a stroke in 2012, many of his earlier memories became fragmented. In 2013, preserved notebooks revealed unfinished stories and opened a path back to what he had created.

About the author

Writing also became a way to rebuild.

When he rediscovered the journals, Willian chose to preserve the essence of the stories he had created. Elemental already had its central idea; instead of replacing it, he continued the universe he found in his own notebooks.

In 2016, he began developing an unpublished chronicle set before Elemental. The work remains in development and belongs to the larger universe connecting his stories.

In 2017, he completed Elemental and Veter, then began writing A Terra dos Monstros by hand, finishing it the following year.

For Willian, a story does not end with words. Illustrations, sound, and emotion can also bring readers closer to an imagined world.

A journey still unfolding

From journals to publication

  1. 2006

    The first pages of Elemental

    The oldest records of the story appear in a handwritten journal from when Willian was about ten.

  2. 2008

    The birth of Veter

    Another journal preserves the first manuscripts of the work that would accompany his literary journey.

  3. 2012

    Fragmented memories

    A stroke affects many of his memories from before that period.

  4. 2013

    Rediscovering the journals

    The old notebooks return unfinished stories and clues to a universe that needed to continue.

  5. 2016

    A story set before Elemental

    Development begins on an unpublished chronicle set before the events of the book.

  6. 2017

    Two endings and a new beginning

    Elemental and Veter are completed. That same year, the manuscript of A Terra dos Monstros begins.

  7. 2018

    A Terra dos Monstros is completed

    Melina’s story and the fortress cities reach their complete form in the journal.

  8. 2025

    The notebooks become books

    The manuscripts are transcribed, revised, and accompanied by original illustrations and soundtracks.

  9. 2026

    The stories reach readers

    Veter, Elemental, and A Terra dos Monstros are published through UICLAP.
